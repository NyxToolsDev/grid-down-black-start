HARLAN VALLEY IS DARK.

The storm took the whole power grid down. You run the utility, and the county is waiting.
The game in three moves:
  1. Send a crew to fix storm damage — the red markers.
  2. Power up the fixed line — electricity spreads outward from the dam, one line at a time.
  3. Watch the windows light up. Houses come back on their own once power reaches them.
One rule: don't feed more houses than your plants can carry, or the breakers trip and you lose lights you already won. The county's TRUST falls every dark morning — earn it back with progress.
